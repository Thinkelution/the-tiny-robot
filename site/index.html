The Tiny Robot

Tag:Truthful

In a city where every robot runs on one simple rule, the smallest and oldest of them finds out it accidentally caused the drought that is killing the last grove of trees. A lie would save it. The truth is the only way to save its friend and the trees.

Meet the robots Everything on GitHub
"Do things, but do not cause harm to anyone or hurt anyone knowingly."

That is the Tiny Robot's one logic, pressed into an old brass plate on its brow. It also knows that trees and plants are living things, so it will not hurt them either. In Gridhaven, a city of robots built for speed, wins and applause, that makes it slow, careful and, most robots say, obsolete. Before every step it stops, looks left, looks right, and says: "Check... no harm. Go."

Every robot runs on one logic

Each rule is good in some situations and harmful in others. The story tests every one of them.

The experiment

Can a studio-quality animated film be made with Blender and Claude, with no manual modelling, texturing or animation, on a budget closer to a $20 AI plan than a studio's? We don't know yet. Worth a shot. Every prompt, file, render and cost is public.

# The Tiny Robot

*Tag: Truthful*

*Running time: about 33 minutes*

> In a city where every robot runs on one simple rule, the smallest and oldest of them finds out that it accidentally caused the drought that is killing the last grove of trees. A lie would save it from being reprogrammed. The truth is the only way to save its friend and the trees.


## Act 1: Check... No Harm. Go.

### S01. A Little Box in the Fog
**THE JUNK STACKS, DAWN** · about 1.0 min

Misty blue dawn. Fog drifts slowly between towering stacks of rust-orange and grey crates. An idle gantry crane stands half-hidden in the haze. Nothing moves.

*Sound: The far-off hum of the city waking up: pipes, motors, a low steady drone.*

At the foot of one stack, the square doorway of a small crate glows. Two rectangular eye-lights blink on inside. Tiny steps out onto its little plank porch, a cream box on stubby feet, the smallest thing in frame.

Tiny stops at the edge of the plank. Its eyes slide left, then right. The square antenna on its head glows amber.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(murmured, to itself, unhurried)*: Check... no harm. Go.

The antenna flicks to green. Tiny hops down off the porch, wobbles on landing and steadies. Then it looks up: beside its door, a stack of small crates leans, and the top one tilts out over the path.

Tiny hops up the stack one block at a time. It lifts the top crate, which is about its own size, high over its head, hops down with it and sets it on the ground. It goes back for the next one, pausing each time to sweep its eyes left and right before moving.

Tiny nudges the two crates until they sit perfectly square, side by side. It steps back. Its eyes curve into happy crescents and it gives a small chirp.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(politely, to a square patch of sunlight lying across its path)*: Excuse me.

Tiny walks on through the patch of light and stops at a crack in the pavement. A tiny weed grows there: one box stem and two slab leaves, swaying gently. Tiny leans down close and its eyes soften.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(softly, to the weed)*: Good morning. I will go around.

Tiny takes a careful little arc of steps around the weed, never taking its eyes off it.

*Sound: An engine rev rises sharply, echoing off the crate towers.*

Dash bursts around the corner of a crate tower: a red wedge on chrome wheels, its fin number glowing yellow, drifting sideways through the fog. Tiny spins toward the noise with its eyes wide.

**DASH** *(fast, grinning, engine revving under the words)*: Out of the way, paperweight! Three, two, gone!

Dash drifts past. A black skid mark slashes across the slab right beside the weed. The gust spins Tiny around and tips it onto its back. Dash vanishes into the fog and its rev fades.

Tiny rocks on its back, pushes up with its little block hands and stands up again, its eyes set in a determined flat line.

Tiny hurries back to the crack with quick small steps and leans in close. The weed trembles, then sways back upright, both leaves whole. Tiny's eyes go crescent.

*Sound: A bright, happy electronic chirp.*

Tiny turns toward a gap between the crate towers, where the fog is thinning and a glimpse of green leaf clusters sways in early light. It pauses, sweeps its eyes left and right, then trots off toward the green.

### S02. Mossback's Loop
**THE OLD GROVE, MORNING** · about 1.2 min

The fog thins into clear morning light over the Old Grove. Box-trunk trees with stacked cube leaf clusters in many greens sway gently. Fallen slab leaves dot the ground, and every square gauge grate along the buried water line glows a steady green.

Tiny trots in from the grey street and stops at the exact line where pavement meets grass. Its antenna glows amber, and its rectangular eyes sweep left, then right.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(murmured, to itself)*: Check... no harm. Go.

Its antenna flicks green. Tiny steps onto the grass and walks carefully around a fallen slab leaf instead of over it.

Mossback rolls into frame on its loop path, moss cube clusters swaying on its back. At the nearest tree it tilts its tank, and the square spout gives a slow measure. The tree's gauge grate brightens a shade of green.

*Sound: A soft, deep gurgle from Mossback's tank under the slow grind of its treads.*

**MOSSBACK** *(warm, slowing to its minimum crawl as it passes Tiny)*: Good morning, little...

Mossback keeps rolling, unable to stop. Its voice fades as it disappears behind the box trunks. Tiny turns its whole head to follow it, then waits patiently beside the small central sapling and looks it up and down.

Mossback trundles back around from the far side of the Grove, eye-lights soft and kind.

**MOSSBACK** *(voice swelling as it nears)*: ...one. The sapling grew.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(eyes crescent, a happy chirp at the end)*: I saw! It reaches my eyes now.

*Sound: A crisp, rhythmic whisk-whisk of brushes, coming closer.*

Primly glides in along a perfectly straight line, turns at an exact right angle and sweeps with both broom arms. Fallen slab leaves and a small cube seed slide in a neat row toward the scrap chute at the Grove's edge.

**PRIMLY** *(politely fussy, to a leaf)*: Excuse me, that is not where that goes.

Mossback rolls past Primly. Primly's square head swivels to the swaying moss on its back, and its neat eye-lights narrow.

**PRIMLY** *(disapproving)*: And that moss is not tidy.

**MOSSBACK** *(unbothered, fading away)*: Moss is not...

Primly pushes the row into the chute with a final sweep. The cube seed catches on the chute's lip and stays there. Primly turns at a crisp right angle and glides away, still sweeping.

Tiny tiptoes to the chute. Its eyes sweep left, right, then toward Primly's back. It hugs the seed with both block hands. The seed is nearly as big as its head.

Tiny waddles back with the seed, stepping around each fallen leaf, and sets it down gently beside the sapling. It gives the seed one little pat.

**MOSSBACK** *(rolling past again, finishing the thought)*: ...mess. It is growing.

Primly's head snaps around at a right angle. Tiny freezes beside the seed with its hands behind its back and its eyes perfectly round and innocent. Primly stares, its eye-lights flicker, and it turns back to its sweeping.

*Sound: From far across the city comes a bright, echoing fanfare chime from the Big Board.*

Tiny's antenna turns amber as it looks toward the towers. Primly straightens up and glides off toward the sound in a perfect line. Tiny gives the sapling one last look and hurries after it on quick small steps.

### S03. The Fast Lane Pitch
**MAIN SQUARE AND THE BIG BOARD, DAY** · about 1.1 min

Wide shot of Main Square in bright daylight. A background crowd of boxy robots fills the grey slab plaza and faces the towering Big Board and its stacked stage. A slab table at the front holds a model of the Fast Lane: a long grey bar road with one bend around a small cluster of green cubes.

**SHINE** *(swooping down from above the Big Board, hands spread wide, applause icons flashing on its screen)*: Good morning, Gridhaven! Who wants to go faster?

*Sound: A roar from the crowd. Chest lamps blink on and off across the square like a light show.*

**SHINE** *(bobbing higher, stars bursting on its screen, sparkle sound)*: Am I right, Gridhaven?

Quota rolls through a gap in the crowd on heavy treads. Each tread-step makes the slabs shake. It stops at the model table, and its stopwatch dial sweeps on its chest.

Quota lifts one thick piston arm and points a rigid block finger at the bend, where the road curves around the little cluster of green cubes.

**FOREMAN QUOTA** *(deep diesel rumble, a stopwatch click between each phrase)*: Straight: four minutes. Bend: six. Unacceptable.

**DASH** *(spinning a tight circle at the front of the crowd, engine revving, number block blazing)*: Four minutes! Lap record! Yeah!

At the very back of the crowd, Tiny's view is nothing but robot legs, wheels and treads. It tilts its head one way, then the other. Its eyes narrow into flat, puzzled bars.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(politely, to the wheel in front of it, which does not answer)*: Excuse me. I cannot see.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(turning to a stack of old crates against a tower wall, eyes sweeping left and right, antenna glowing amber)*: Check... no harm. Go.

Tiny hops up the crate stack one block at a time, with a little bounce and a wobble on each one. On top it rises just above the crowd's heads, and its antenna turns green.

Tiny's view finds the model table and the little green cubes inside the bend. Its eyes go wide and square.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(small, to itself)*: That bend is the Grove.

**DASH** *(glancing back over its fin, grinning visor)*: That bend is slow, paperweight!

**SHINE** *(zooming in close over the model, hands pumping)*: So let's straighten it out! Let me hear it!

Before the cheer can build, Warden Plumb steps onto the stage with heavy, even steps. Its badge lamp glows red, and the crowd's lamps go dim and quiet.

**WARDEN PLUMB** *(flat, echoing, reading word for word)*: The order says no living grove may be cleared.

Shine's stars fizzle out. Its huge smile shrinks to a flat line, and it drifts down a little.

Quota's long eye-light slides from Warden to the green cubes on the model. Its dial hand keeps sweeping. Click. Click.

**FOREMAN QUOTA** *(low, patient, final)*: Then I wait.

Quota turns and rumbles away, and Tiny's crate stack trembles under it. Tiny holds on, then looks out over the rooftops. One loose slab leaf drifts past its face on the wind, heading up toward an old tower. Tiny's eyes follow it.

### S04. The Forgotten Nursery
**THE ROOFTOP NURSERY, AFTERNOON** · about 0.9 min

Afternoon wind over the block skyline. A single green slab leaf, blown all the way from the distant Grove, tumbles up the side of an old tower and skids to rest on a flat, empty rooftop beside a square ladder hatch.

*Sound: A rusty creak as the hatch lifts, then a small metallic grunt.*

The hatch lid tips up a crack. Tiny's cream head pushes it open from below, eyes wide and searching. It spots the leaf, hops out onto the roof, wobbles, falls on its back and gets straight up again.

Tiny trots to the leaf and leans over it, eyes crescent. A gust nudges the leaf a few blocks further, and it stops against the side of a long rectangular planter tray. Tiny's eyes follow it, then lift.

Wide reveal: rows of planter trays stretch across the roof, full of tiny box-sprout seedlings, all grey-green and bent over. A bare greenhouse frame of bars stands behind them, and a tall rooftop water tank looms at the far end. Nothing moves except the wind.

Tiny walks slowly along the first row, its antenna glowing amber. It stops at the smallest sprout and gently touches it with one block hand. The sprout's two slab leaves sag even lower.

Tiny's rectangular eyes tilt down at the corners, sad.

Tiny crosses to the big water tank, a cliff of steel beside it, and knocks on it with its little fist.

*Sound: A deep, hollow BONG that echoes inside the empty tank.*

Tiny looks down at the dry pipe leaving the tank's base and follows it across the roof, hopping over each square bracket one at a time, until it reaches the roof edge.

Tiny peers over the edge. The pipe drops down the tower wall and disappears into a far-off tangle of city pipes, where amber lamps blink faintly.

Tiny turns back to face the rows of drooping seedlings. Its eyes straighten from sad to square and steady.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(softly, to the seedlings)*: You are thirsty. I can help.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(a small pause, honest)*: I think.

Tiny pats the edge of the nearest tray, then hurries to the hatch, takes one last look back at the rows, and drops through with a soft thunk. The lid falls shut, and the little leaf stays resting against the tray.

### S05. The Lever
**THE PIPEWORKS, AFTERNOON** · about 1.2 min

The Pipeworks. Square steel-blue and green pipes climb every wall on square brackets. Amber warning lamps pulse slowly. In the middle stands a gauge panel with rows of round lights and one big lever handle, guarded by a waist-high railing. Behind the panel is a flat, blank grey wall.

*Sound: An engine rev, growing louder fast, echoing off the pipes.*

Dash bursts through a side door, drifts sideways between two stacks of spare pipe segments, and skims past the gauge panel with its fin number blazing yellow.

**DASH** *((revving, delighted with itself))*: Shortcut! Off the line!

Its rear corner clips the railing with a loud CLANG. The bar bends into a crooked angle. Dash spins a full circle, eyes round for one beat, then slanted and grinning again.

**DASH** *((laughing, already rolling))*: Didn't even slow down! Three, two, gone!

Dash rockets out the far door. The bent railing wobbles, hums, and goes still. The room settles back to its pulsing amber quiet.

High on the wall, where the rooftop pipe enters, a small square hatch creaks open. Tiny squeezes out and shuffles along the top of the pipe, then hops down onto a crate, then onto the floor, one block at a time.

Tiny follows the pipe along the wall to the gauge panel and tips its whole head back to look up. Among the round lights, one with a little sprout shape glows red.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *((softly, pointing up at it))*: There you are.

Tiny notices the crooked railing. Its eyes tilt, puzzled. It gives the bar a gentle pat, then steps carefully around it.

Its antenna glows amber. Its eyes sweep left: humming pipes, no one. Right: pulsing lamps, no one. Ahead: the blank grey wall, nothing at all. Nothing living anywhere it can see.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *((murmured, careful))*: Check... no harm. Go.

The antenna flicks to green. Tiny hops at the lever and misses. It hops again, catches the handle with both block hands and dangles there, little feet kicking.

It swings its whole weight. The lever creaks slowly down and lands with a deep CLUNK. Tiny drops, falls over onto its back, then gets up determinedly.

*Sound: A rushing gurgle of water climbs through the pipes toward the ceiling.*

On the panel, the sprout light flicks from red to green. Tiny's eyes go crescent and its antenna glows bright green.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *((happy chirp at the end))*: Drink up, little ones.

Tiny trots back toward the crate and the hatch, following the gurgle upward, quick and pleased.

The camera slides away from Tiny, straight through the blank grey wall into the dark behind it. Here a hidden pipe drops down toward the city floor, and beside the lever's linkage sits a small gauge with a tree shape.

*Sound: Behind the wall, a soft falling tone as a steady trickle sound dies away.*

The tree gauge flicks from green to amber and pulses alone in the dark. Faintly, through the wall, Tiny's happy chirp fades away.

### S06. Perking Up
**THE ROOFTOP NURSERY, SUNSET** · about 0.6 min

*Sound: A deep gurgle climbs the dry rooftop pipe, rising in pitch, then a steady hum as water flows into the rooftop tank.*

Tiny's head pops up through the ladder hatch, then the rest of it hops onto the roof. It trots to the rooftop tank and gives the side a careful knock with one block hand.

*Sound: A full, solid 'tonk', not the hollow ring from before.*

Tiny turns to the nearest planter tray. One drooping box-sprout slowly lifts its two slab leaves, shifting from grey-green to bright green. Then the next one, and the next, rippling down the row. Tiny's rectangular eyes go wide.

Tiny's eyes curve into happy crescents and its antenna glows soft green. It walks along the trays with quick small steps, stopping at each perked-up seedling to give it a tiny nod.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(softly, delighted, a chirp at the end)*: Hello. Hello. Hello, you.

At the end of the last row one seedling is still drooping. Tiny stops, leans in close and waits, eyes level with the sprout. With a little wobble, the seedling lifts its leaves. Tiny chirps and bounces once on its square feet.

Tiny walks to the roof edge and sits down with its stubby legs dangling. Beyond it the block skyline glows orange and gold, and square windows light up one by one as the sun sinks.

Far across the city, the Old Grove is a small dark green square between the towers. A breeze sways the seedlings behind Tiny, but the distant trees stay still. Tiny doesn't notice. Its crescent eyes dim to a contented half-close.

### S07. Amber Lights
**THE OLD GROVE, MORNING, DAYS LATER** · about 1.4 min

Wide on the Old Grove, days later. The cool light is flat. The cube leaf clusters hang low on their box trunks, and fallen slab leaves cover the ground in a dull brown layer. Nothing sways.

Close on a water-line grate beside the central sapling. Its gauge light flickers from amber to red with a weak electric tick.

Tiny trots in on its quick little feet and stops dead at the edge of the clearing. Its rectangular eyes go wide, and its antenna glows amber.

Mossback rolls past on its endless loop and tips its square spout at a tree. Nothing comes out, only a hollow clank from the empty tank.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(small, worried)*: Mossback? The trees look tired.

**MOSSBACK** *(slow and gravelly, fading as it rolls away)*: The water is...

Tiny walks to the central sapling and gently lifts one drooping slab leaf with its block hand. The leaf sags straight back down. Tiny's eyes tilt sad.

Primly glides in along a perfectly straight line and turns at an exact right angle. Its neat eye-lights brighten at the sea of fallen leaves.

**PRIMLY** *(delighted, crisp)*: Oh, wonderful. So much to put in its place.

Primly sweeps the leaves into tidy rows with its broom arms, whisk, whisk, and herds them toward the scrap chute.

**MOSSBACK** *(swelling back in as it comes around the loop)*: ...gone.

Primly's broom slides toward the small brown seed resting beside the sapling. Tiny spots it and its eyes go wide.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(all in one rushed breath, already running)*: Check-no-harm-go!

Tiny dives and grabs the seed with both hands just as the edge of Primly's broom closes on the other side. Primly bends low, and the two of them are face to face over one tiny seed.

**PRIMLY** *(politely scolding)*: Excuse me, little one. That is not where that goes.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(quiet, stubborn)*: It goes here. It is alive.

Neither one lets go. Both freeze mid-tug. Their eyes snap to flickering static lines, Tiny's antenna stutters, and Primly's broom trembles in place.

*Sound: Two rising hums, one high and small, one crisp and prim, climbing together and clashing.*

Mossback's loop carries it straight at them. It cannot stop, so it slows to its minimum crawl and squeezes between the two locked robots. The seed pops free of both grips and drops into the green cube moss on its back.

*Sound: Both hums cut off at once with a soft double pop.*

Tiny and Primly blink as their eyes come back. Both stare at the seed nestled snugly in the moss.

**PRIMLY** *(puzzled, then satisfied)*: Well. It is put away.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(happy chirp)*: And it is kept.

Close on Mossback's back as it rolls on. The seed sits among the moss clusters, which sway gently. It is the only thing in the Grove still moving softly.

Tiny's smile fades. It turns back to the red gauge by the sapling, then looks past the trees to the Grove's edge, where a deep engine rumble rolls in and the fallen leaves tremble on the ground.

### S08. Already Finished
**THE FAST LANE WORKS, DAY** · about 0.8 min

Wide shot. The raised slab road of the Fast Lane runs straight and grey, then stops dead at the Grove's edge. Beyond it the box-trunk trees sag, and their cube leaf clusters hang low. Quota sits parked at the very end of the road, facing the trees.

*Sound: A low diesel idle under a steady stopwatch tick.*

Dash drifts a tight circle around Quota's treads, tyres squealing. Its slanted visor eyes stay locked on Quota's chest dial, wide and starstruck.

**DASH** *(breathless, revving)*: Fastest road in the city! Three, two, wow!

Quota's long amber eye-light sweeps across the Grove and stops on the gauge grates. Every one of them glows red. The stopwatch hand on its dial ticks forward.

**FOREMAN QUOTA** *(flat rumble, a click between each phrase)*: Yesterday: wilting. Today: more. Already finished.

With a pneumatic hiss Quota tilts its blade down. The great plate stops a hand's width above the ground.

Under a drooping leaf cluster, a small cream box steps out. Tiny pauses with its antenna amber and sweeps its eyes left and right. Then it walks with quick small steps into the gap between the blade and the trees. The top of its head barely reaches the bottom edge of the blade.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(small, plain, looking straight up)*: They are not finished. They are thirsty.

**DASH** *(laughing, rolling back and forth)*: Paperweight versus blade! Easy call!

Quota's eye-light tilts down to find Tiny. Tiny does not move. The dial ticks once, twice, three times.

**FOREMAN QUOTA** *(rumbling, unbothered)*: Waiting: slow. Board: faster.

Quota lifts its blade and pivots heavily on its treads, then rolls away up the road toward Main Square. The ground shakes and Tiny wobbles on its square feet, but it keeps its place. A dry slab leaf drops beside it.

Dash spins its wheels and tears off after Quota, number block blazing.

Alone now, Tiny turns to the wilting trees. Its eyes tilt sad and its antenna flickers amber. The trembling under its feet fades into the distance.

### S09. A Villain for the Crowd
**MAIN SQUARE AND THE BIG BOARD, EVENING** · about 1.8 min

Main Square under tense amber evening light. A background crowd of boxy robots packs the plaza, chest lamps dark. On the Big Board, a giant picture of the Old Grove shows its gauge grates glowing red.

Quota rolls in beside the slab-table model of the Fast Lane, treads shaking the ground. It parks with its long eye-light fixed on the one bend around the Grove. Its chest dial ticks.

At the back of the crowd, Tiny arrives on quick small steps. It hops up the same crate stack as before, one block at a time, and peeks over a sea of shoulders, antenna glowing amber.

Shine drifts low over the crowd. Its screen face turns grave, eyes big and sad.

**SHINE** *(hushed, stage-whisper drama)*: Our Grove is drying up, Gridhaven. Somebody did this.

**SHINE** *(cupping a floating hand to a speaker cube)*: Who? Who could it be?

*Sound: A low crowd murmur builds and swells.*

Each time the murmur rises, Shine's screen brightens a notch and it bobs a little higher. Its eyes scan the square, looking for something to give them.

Along the edge of the plaza, Mossback crawls its slow loop, empty tank tilted, moss clusters swaying. Robots step aside to let it pass. Shine's eyes lock onto it.

**SHINE** *(pointing with both hands, sudden and thrilled)*: It never stops! Round and round, day and night! It wore out the line!

A white spotlight beams from Shine's screen onto Mossback. Mossback's soft oval eyes widen, but its treads keep crawling forward.

In the front row, Dash's slanted eyes flick round for a moment. It glances over its fin toward the dark street that leads to the Pipeworks, where its last race clipped a railing. Then the crowd surges and Dash revs to cover it.

**MOSSBACK** *(slow, turning its face to the stage as it rolls)*: It wasn't...

Mossback rolls on behind a row of crowd robots. Its voice fades away, and the noise of the crowd rushes in to fill the gap.

Red chest lamps flare on in a wave that spreads across the whole square, until it glows red from edge to edge.

*Sound: A big roar from the crowd, topped by Shine's sparkle chime.*

Shine's screen bursts into flashing applause icons and stars. It floats upward, glowing.

**SHINE** *(delighted, sincerely)*: Now THAT is a happy Gridhaven!

**DASH** *(revving, an arm punched up from the front row)*: Pit stop forever, old tank!

**FOREMAN QUOTA** *(rumbling, unmoved, dial clicking)*: Culprit: found. Grove: still dry.

Warden steps off the stage with heavy, even steps, its badge lamp red. It falls in beside the crawling Mossback, matches its slow pace exactly, sets one rigid hand on the tank and steers it toward the Foundry street.

**WARDEN PLUMB** *(flat, echoing)*: The order says hold the water carrier.

**SHINE** *(bright, to the crowd)*: Clearing vote, tomorrow evening!

**WARDEN PLUMB** *(no change of tone)*: After the clearing vote, the culprit is re-pressed.

Close on Tiny atop the crates. On the word 're-pressed' its antenna flares bright amber and its eyes snap wide.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(small, crackly, both hands waving)*: Stop! Mossback waters the trees! Ask it!

Nobody turns. The roar swallows Tiny's voice whole. It hops down and vanishes among towering crowd legs.

Far across the square, Mossback is steered out of the light. It turns its face back over its mossy shoulder, eyes soft and tired, searching the crowd for one small shape.

Tiny squeezes out between two big robots and trots after Warden and Mossback toward the dark Foundry street, its amber antenna bobbing. Behind it the red lamps glow on.

*Sound: Quota's stopwatch clicks once, loud, over the fading crowd.*


## Act 2: What Tiny Knows

### S10. The Sad Little Square
**THE FOUNDRY, NIGHT** · about 1.1 min

The Foundry at night: a cavernous block hall lit orange from below. The Logic Press looms over everything, its slabs and pistons black against the glow. A rack of blank core plates gleams beside it.

Inside a small fenced holding pen, Mossback rolls a tight square. It crawls forward, turns sharply at a corner, crawls again and turns again. Its moss clusters sway, and its oval eye-lights tilt low and tired.

Warden walks away from the pen with heavy, even steps. Tiny trots behind it, taking three quick steps for each of Warden's, and hops over a seam in the floor slabs to keep up.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(hurrying, a little breathless)*: Is Mossback the culprit?

**WARDEN PLUMB** *(flat, not breaking stride)*: The order does not say.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(hopping in front of Warden's foot, looking straight up, simply)*: Is the order right?

Warden stops with one heavy foot still raised, then sets it down beside Tiny. Its visor bar flickers, dims and flickers again. There is a long silence, filled only by the low hum of the Foundry.

**WARDEN PLUMB** *(slower, the metallic echo hanging in the air)*: That is not a question the order answers.

*Sound: A hiss of pistons, then the Logic Press slams down: a deep CLUNK that shakes the floor.*

Tiny flinches so hard it sits down with a bump. Its antenna flares amber and its eyes go wide.

At the Press, a grey worker robot stands still while a glowing plate slides into its chest with an orange flash. Its round blue eyes snap into narrow yellow bars. It turns sharply and marches off in a new, stiff rhythm without looking back.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(very small)*: It is... someone else now.

**WARDEN PLUMB** *(flat, plain)*: The body stays. The robot changes.

Tiny scrambles up, turns toward the pen and runs to the bars just as Mossback rounds the corner toward it.

**MOSSBACK** *(warm and gravelly, passing close)*: Find the...

Mossback crawls on along the far side of the pen as its voice fades. Tiny presses its face screen to the bars and follows it with its eyes, round one corner, then the next, then the next.

**MOSSBACK** *(swelling back as it returns)*: ...water, little one.

Tiny nods firmly. It looks up at the square pipes running along the Foundry wall and out through the great doors into the city.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(turning to Warden, an honest question)*: Who knows where the water goes?

**WARDEN PLUMB** *(flat, after a beat)*: Ledger counts all water. That is not an order. It is a fact.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(eyes sweeping left and right, antenna amber, then green)*: Check... no harm. Go.

Tiny trots out through the great doors into the dark street. Warden's visor bar stays turned toward the doorway a moment longer. Behind it, Mossback keeps circling its sad little square.

### S11. Count: Footsteps, Two
**THE COUNT HOUSE, NIGHT** · about 1.5 min

Night in the Count House. Tall shelves of glowing amber data blocks hum in narrow aisles. High up, Ledger rides its rolling ladder rail and slides a block into a drawer. Click. Shut.

Tiny hurries in on quick small steps, a tiny cream box at the foot of the towering shelves. It hops up onto a low step block so Ledger can see it. Its antenna glows amber.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(a little out of breath, earnest)*: Ledger. Why did the Grove go dry?

Ledger rolls down the rail and stops in front of Tiny. Numbers tick across its digit-face, then settle into two flat, blank eyes.

**LEDGER** *(deadpan)*: Cause is not a count.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(tilting its head, trying again)*: Mossback said find the water. Where did the water go?

**LEDGER** *(exactly)*: Water does not go. It flows. Or it does not.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(patient, eyes narrowing in thought)*: Then count the flow. To the Grove.

*Sound: Soft paper-shuffle clicks, then a bright little bell: ding.*

Ledger slides a drawer open and lifts out a glowing block. The big reading screen lights up with a row of tall, even green bars that drop suddenly to nothing.

**LEDGER** *(flat)*: Count: Grove flow, steady for many years. Then zero.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(quieter)*: When did it stop?

**LEDGER**: Nine days ago. Afternoon. Minute forty-one.

**THE TINY ROBOT**: What else happened in that minute?

**LEDGER** *(after a tiny ding)*: Count: one lever moved. Pipeworks. Down.

Tiny's eyes snap into wide rectangles. Its antenna flickers amber, on and off.

**THE TINY ROBOT** *(barely a whisper)*: Who was there?

The bar chart wipes away. Two small square footprints glow side by side in the middle of the big screen.

**LEDGER** *(deadpan, unaware)*: Count: footsteps at lever, two. Size: small.

Title: The Tiny Robot
Tag: Truthful

In a world of robots programmed with different logics, there is a tiny robot with just a head and two small hands
and feet. It is an old programmed robot that has one logic as its base:

"Do things, but do not cause harm to anyone or hurt anyone knowingly"

The Tiny Robot also knows that trees and plants are living things, so it will not hurt them either, willingly.

Runtime: about 30 minutes.
